# Named AI Platforms for Tamil Translation and Dubbing

## Verified platform findings

| Platform | Officially verified product scope | Tamil translation / dubbing conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Descript | Its dubbing help page explicitly lists **Tamil** among stock-voice dubbing targets. The documented workflow translates content, enables **Dub speech**, maps each speaker label to a stock voice, and can regenerate dubbed audio. | A strong practical video-dubbing candidate when editing, speaker assignment, and regenerated voiceovers matter. |
| Murf AI / Murf Dub | Murf's Tamil dubbing page describes context-aware translation, audio dubbing, lip-movement adaptation, and native-speaker/linguist review options. | A strong candidate for Tamil dubbing and voice-over work, subject to testing the actual Tamil voice and plan limits. |
| Suno | The official homepage describes a product for creating, editing, remixing, and producing original **songs**. | Not a video speech translation or dubbing platform. It can be useful only for creating Tamil music, lyrics, or background tracks. |
| Udio | The official site identifies Udio as an **AI Music Generator**. | Not a video speech translation or dubbing platform. It may create Tamil-style songs but should not be used to translate spoken dialogue into Tamil. |
